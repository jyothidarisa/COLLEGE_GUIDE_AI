# Chandigarh University (CU-PUNJAB)
Location: Mohali, Punjab
NIRF Engineering Rank: #31
Category / College Type: Private University
Official Website: https://www.cuchd.in/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: CUCET / JEE Main (programme dependent)
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 50
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with required subjects; programme-specific criteria apply
- Admission Modes & Counseling: University admission process; Counseling Authority: Chandigarh University counselling
- Admission Process: Online application, entrance/merit selection, counselling, document verification, fee payment and admission confirmation
- Important Documents Required: Class 10 certificate, Class 12 marksheet, entrance-exam score/rank card, ID proof, photographs, category certificate if applicable, domicile certificate if applicable, medical certificate and allotment/admission letter
