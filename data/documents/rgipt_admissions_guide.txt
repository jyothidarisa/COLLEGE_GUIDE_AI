# Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology (RGIPT)
Location: Amethi, Uttar Pradesh
NIRF Engineering Rank: #78
Category / College Type: Institute of National Importance
Official Website: https://www.rgipt.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Main, JEE Advanced
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 75% (65% SC/ST/PwD)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics; qualified in JEE Advanced
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Advanced + JoSAA Counselling; Counseling Authority: JoSAA Counselling
- Admission Process: JEE Advanced -> JoSAA Registration -> Choice Filling -> Seat Allotment -> Reporting
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JEE Advanced Scorecard, JoSAA Allotment Letter, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹225,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹225,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹225,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Hostel Establishment INR 8,000/sem + Seat Rent INR 8,000/sem (approx. INR 16,000/sem)
- Room Types Available: Single, Double Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 80%
- Highest Package: INR 24 LPA
- Average Package: INR 11 LPA
- Top Recruiters: ONGC, Halliburton, Shell, Oil India Limited (OIL), Schlumberger
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Institute Merit Scholarship, SC/ST Fee Waiver, MCM Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: Fee waivers for SC/ST/PwD candidates and merit-cum-means assistance for economically weaker students as per MoE norms.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 200 acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Nihalgarh Railway Station (approx. 8 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Amethi Bus Stand (approx. 10 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: ONGC, OIL, GAIL, Indian Oil Corporation
