# Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology (VELTECH)
Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu
NIRF Engineering Rank: #87
Category / College Type: Private Deemed University
Official Website: https://www.veltech.edu.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: VTUEEE, JEE Main, TNEA
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 50% aggregate
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: Entrance Test Based (VTUEEE) + TNEA/JEE Main considered; Counseling Authority: Vel Tech Counselling
- Admission Process: Apply Online -> VTUEEE/JEE/TNEA Score -> Counselling -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, VTUEEE/JEE/TNEA Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹265,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹225,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹200,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 1,05,000-2,00,000 per year
- Room Types Available: Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 80%
- Highest Package: INR 24 LPA
- Average Package: INR 4 LPA
- Top Recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, HCL
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: VTUEEE Merit Scholarship, Sports Scholarship, SAARC Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: Merit-based fee concessions for top VTUEEE/JEE rank holders; SAARC students exempt from institution and hostel caution deposit.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: Avadi Campus - approx. 50 acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Avadi Railway Station (approx. 2 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Avadi Bus Stand (approx. 1 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: DRDO, ISRO-affiliated research projects, TCS
