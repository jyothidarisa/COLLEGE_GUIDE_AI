# Chitkara University (Punjab Campus) (CHITKARA)
Location: Rajpura, Punjab
NIRF Engineering Rank: #89
Category / College Type: Private University
Official Website: https://www.chitkara.edu.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: CUCET/CMST, JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 55% aggregate
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: Entrance Test Based (Chitkara Merit Scholarship Test) + JEE Main considered; Counseling Authority: Chitkara University Counselling
- Admission Process: Apply Online -> CMST/JEE Score -> Counselling -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, CMST/JEE Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹475,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹350,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹300,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 1,10,000-1,75,000 per semester (varies by branch/room type)
- Room Types Available: Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 92%
- Highest Package: INR 1.1 Crore
- Average Package: INR 12.36 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Amazon, Infosys, TCS, NVIDIA
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Chitkara Merit Scholarship, Sports Scholarship, Sibling Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: Merit-based tuition fee waivers up to 100% for top CMST/JEE scorers, plus sibling and sports fee concessions.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: Rajpura Campus - approx. 100+ acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Rajpura Railway Station (approx. 5 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Rajpura Bus Stand (approx. 6 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: NVIDIA, Amazon, IBM (Centres of Excellence)
