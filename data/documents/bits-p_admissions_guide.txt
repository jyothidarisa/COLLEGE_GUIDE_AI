# Birla Institute of Technology & Science -Pilani (BITS-P)
Location: Pilani, Rajasthan
NIRF Engineering Rank: #11
Category / College Type: Deemed University (Private)
Official Website: https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: BITSAT
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 75% overall in Class 12, 60% in PCM
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics; minimum 75% aggregate
- Admission Modes & Counseling: BITSAT Score-based, Direct Admission; Counseling Authority: BITS Admission Cell
- Admission Process: BITSAT Exam → Merit List → Online Choice Filling → Seat Allotment → Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, BITSAT Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Migration Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Passport-size Photos, Medical Fitness Certificate

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹232,500
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹232,500
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹232,500
- Hostel Accommodation Details: 120000
- Room Types Available: Double, Triple; Attached Bathrooms: yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 89%
- Highest Package: ₹1.47 Crore per annum
- Average Package: 22.28 LPA (median 19-20 LPA, combined campuses)
- Top Recruiters: DE Shaw, Amazon, Google, Microsoft, JP Morgan, Adobe, Cisco, McKinsey, BCG, Deloitte
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit Scholarship, Need-based Scholarship, Connect Scholar Program, SC/ST Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: Tuition fee waivers ranging from 25% to 100% based on merit and family income, plus need-based financial assistance
