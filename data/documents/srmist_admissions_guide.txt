# S.R.M. Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST)
Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu
NIRF Engineering Rank: #14
Category / College Type: Deemed University (Private)
Official Website: https://www.srmist.edu.in/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: SRMJEEE / JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 60% overall in Class 12
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: SRMJEEE, JEE Main Score, Direct Admission; Counseling Authority: SRM Admission Cell
- Admission Process: SRMJEEE / JEE Main Score → Counselling → Seat Allotment → Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, Entrance Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Passport-size Photos, Medical Fitness Certificate

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹285,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹285,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹275,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: 140000
- Room Types Available: Double, Triple, Four-sharing; Attached Bathrooms: yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 91%
- Highest Package: ₹57 Lakhs
- Average Package: 7.3 LPA (median 6.5 LPA)
- Top Recruiters: Amazon, TCS, Accenture, Wipro, Google, PayPal, Microsoft, Infosys, Cognizant, HCL
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit Scholarship, Sports Scholarship, SRM Scholarship for Rank Holders, Sibling Discount
- Scholarship Details: Tuition fee waivers of 10-100% for top rankers in SRMJEEE/JEE Main, plus sports and defence-quota concessions
