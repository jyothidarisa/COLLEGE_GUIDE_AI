# Atal Bihari Vajpayee Indian Institute of Information Technology and Management (ABV-IIITM) Gwalior (IIITM-GWL)
Location: Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh
NIRF Engineering Rank: #96
Category / College Type: Institute of National Importance
Official Website: https://www.iiitm.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Main, DASA
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 75% (65% SC/ST/PwD)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Main + JoSAA/CSAB Counselling (also DASA for foreign nationals); Counseling Authority: JoSAA/CSAB Counselling
- Admission Process: JEE Main -> JoSAA Registration -> Choice Filling -> Seat Allotment -> Reporting
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JEE Main Scorecard, JoSAA Allotment Letter, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹158,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹158,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹158,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 37,000 per year
- Room Types Available: Shared (1st year), Single/Double based on CGPA (senior years); Attached Bathrooms: No
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 74%
- Highest Package: INR 67.5 LPA
- Average Package: INR 27.23 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Google, Uber, CRED, Deloitte, Dell, American Express, Microsoft, Amazon
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: SC/ST Fee Waiver, Merit-cum-Means Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: 100% tuition waiver for SC/ST/PwD candidates; income-based remission for economically weaker students.

- Campus Area: 160+ acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Gwalior Railway Station (approx. 10 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Gwalior Bus Stand (approx. 10 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: Google, Microsoft, American Express (recruitment partnerships)
