# Jain University (Faculty of Engineering & Technology, Jain Global Campus) (JAIN-BLR)
Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka
NIRF Engineering Rank: #84
Category / College Type: Private Deemed University
Official Website: https://jainuniversity.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JET, JEE Main, COMEDK, KCET
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics, plus Chemistry/Biology/CS
- Admission Modes & Counseling: Entrance Test Based (JET) + JEE Main/COMEDK/KCET considered; Counseling Authority: Jain University Counselling
- Admission Process: Apply Online -> JET/JEE/COMEDK Score -> Counselling -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JET/JEE/COMEDK Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹325,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹300,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹225,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 1,80,000-2,20,000 per year
- Room Types Available: Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 85%
- Highest Package: INR 60 LPA
- Average Package: INR 6.5 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Tata Technologies, Texas Instruments, SIEMENS, TCS, Infosys
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: JET Merit Scholarship, JEE/COMEDK Rank Scholarship, Sports Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: 25% first-year fee concession for high JET/JEE/COMEDK scorers; additional sports and defence-quota scholarships available.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: Jain Global Campus - approx. 75 acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Kengeri Railway Station (approx. 10 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Kanakapura Road Bus Stand (approx. 5 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: Tata Technologies, Texas Instruments, SIEMENS
