# Koneru Lakshmaiah Education Foundation (KLEF)
Location: Guntur, Andhra Pradesh
NIRF Engineering Rank: #35
Category / College Type: Deemed University (Private)
Official Website: https://www.kluniversity.in/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: KLEEE / JEE Main (programme dependent)
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 60
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with PCM; programme-specific criteria apply
- Admission Modes & Counseling: University admission process; Counseling Authority: KLU counselling
- Admission Process: Online application, entrance/merit selection, counselling, document verification, fee payment and admission confirmation
- Important Documents Required: Class 10 certificate, Class 12 marksheet, entrance-exam score/rank card, ID proof, photographs, category certificate if applicable, domicile certificate if applicable, medical certificate and allotment/admission letter
