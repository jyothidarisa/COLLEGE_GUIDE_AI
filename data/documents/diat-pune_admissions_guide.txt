# Defence Institute of Advanced Technology (DIAT) (DIAT-PUNE)
Location: Pune, Maharashtra
NIRF Engineering Rank: #92
Category / College Type: Deemed University (Ministry of Defence)
Official Website: https://www.diat.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Main, GATE
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 75% (65% SC/ST/PwD)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (B.Tech); BE/B.Tech with 55% (M.Tech)
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Main (B.Tech) / GATE (M.Tech) Based; Counseling Authority: JoSAA Counselling (B.Tech) / CCMT (M.Tech)
- Admission Process: JEE Main/GATE -> JoSAA/CCMT Registration -> Choice Filling -> Seat Allotment -> Reporting
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JEE Main/GATE Scorecard, Category Certificate, Photo ID, Migration Certificate

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹270,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹270,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹270,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 2,60,000-3,90,000 for full programme duration
- Room Types Available: Single, Double Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 90%
- Highest Package: INR 18 LPA
- Average Package: INR 9-16 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Philips, Mahindra Defence, Tech Mahindra, TCS, Intel, Bharat Electronics, Bosch, L&T
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: DRDO Scholarship, Defence Wards Scholarship, SC/ST Fee Waiver
- Scholarship Details: Fee concessions for wards of defence personnel and DRDO-sponsored scholars; SC/ST/PwD tuition waivers as per MoE norms.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 496 acres (Girinagar, near Khadakwasla Dam)
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Pune Junction (approx. 15 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Swargate Bus Stand (approx. 10 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: DRDO, Bharat Electronics Limited, GE Aerospace
