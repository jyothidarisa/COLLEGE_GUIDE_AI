# SR University (SRU)
Location: Warangal, Telangana
NIRF Engineering Rank: #91
Category / College Type: Private University
Official Website: https://sru.edu.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: TS EAMCET, JEE Main, SRSAT
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 55% (50% for reserved category)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: TS EAMCET/JEE Main + SRSAT considered; Counseling Authority: SR University Counselling / TS EAPCET Counselling
- Admission Process: Apply Online -> EAMCET/JEE/SRSAT Score -> Counselling -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, EAMCET/JEE/SRSAT Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹175,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹150,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹125,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 85,000-1,30,000 per year including mess
- Room Types Available: AC/Non-AC Single, Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 92%
- Highest Package: INR 51 LPA
- Average Package: INR 6.5 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Microsoft, Salesforce, Flipkart, Infosys, Cognizant, Accenture
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit Scholarship, Founders Scholarship, Telangana Fee Reimbursement
- Scholarship Details: Merit scholarships for top TS EAMCET/JEE/SRSAT rank holders (25-100% tuition waiver); Founders Scholarship for financially disadvantaged students.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 150 acres (Ananthasagar, Hasanparthy)
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Warangal Railway Station (approx. 12 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Hasanparthy Bus Stand (approx. 2 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: Microsoft, Salesforce, Berger
