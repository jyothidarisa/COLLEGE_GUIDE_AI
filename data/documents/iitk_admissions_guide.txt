# Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (IITK)
Location: Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh
NIRF Engineering Rank: #4
Category / College Type: Institute of National Importance
Official Website: https://www.iitk.ac.in/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Advanced
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 75% overall in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST/PwD)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics; qualified in JEE Advanced
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Advanced, JoSAA Counselling; Counseling Authority: JoSAA
- Admission Process: JEE Advanced → JoSAA Counselling → Seat Allotment → Document Verification → Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JEE Advanced Scorecard, JoSAA Allotment Letter, Transfer Certificate, Migration Certificate, Category/PwD Certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar Card, Passport-size Photos, Medical Fitness Certificate

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹220,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹220,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹220,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: 80000
- Room Types Available: Single, Double, Triple; Attached Bathrooms: Partial
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 93%
- Highest Package: ₹1.9 Crore per annum (domestic, 2023-24 figure)
- Average Package: 19.4-23 LPA (median, NIRF 2025)
- Top Recruiters: Microsoft, Sprinklr, Samsung, Qualcomm, Adobe, Goldman Sachs, Rubrik, Reliance, Bajaj Auto, TCS
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit Scholarship, Merit-cum-Means (MCM) Scholarship, Institute Free Studentship, SC/ST/OBC Scholarship, EWS Scholarship, National Scholarships
- Scholarship Details: Tuition fee waivers and stipends based on merit, category and family income; MCM scholarship of Rs 1000-2000/month for eligible students

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 1055 acres
- Facilities: Library (yes), Research Labs (yes), Gym (yes), Medical (yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Kanpur Central Railway Station (15 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Kanpur Bus Stand (16 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), 24×7 Campus Security, CCTV Surveillance, Women's Hostel Security, Anti-Sexual Harassment Cell, Emergency Helpline), Medical Support (24×7 Campus/Institute Hospital, Ambulance Service, Emergency Medical Care, Resident Doctors, Pharmacy, Counselling Services)
- Industry Collaborations: Microsoft, IBM, Intel, Samsung, Cisco, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, L&T, Bosch
