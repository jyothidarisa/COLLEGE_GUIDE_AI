# Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering and Technology (SLIET)
Location: Sangrur, Punjab
NIRF Engineering Rank: #79
Category / College Type: Deemed University (Institute of National Importance)
Official Website: https://sliet.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 60% aggregate
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Main + JoSAA/CSAB Counselling; Counseling Authority: JoSAA/CSAB Counselling
- Admission Process: JEE Main -> JoSAA/CSAB Registration -> Choice Filling -> Seat Allotment -> Reporting
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JEE Main Scorecard, Category Certificate, Photo ID, Migration Certificate

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹400,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹400,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹400,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 25,000-35,000 per year including mess
- Room Types Available: Single, Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: No
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 70%
- Highest Package: INR 23 LPA
- Average Package: INR 3.5-4.5 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Walmart, Godrej, Byju's, Mahindra Tech, Infosys
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: SC/ST Scholarship, Merit Scholarship, Post-Matric Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: Government post-matric scholarships and institute merit awards for SC/ST/OBC and top-performing students.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 451 acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Sangrur Railway Station (approx. 25 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Longowal Bus Stand (approx. 1 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: Mahindra Tech, Infosys, National Skill Development Corporation
