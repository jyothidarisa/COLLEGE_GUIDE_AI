# COEP Technological University (formerly College of Engineering Pune) (COEP)
Location: Pune, Maharashtra
NIRF Engineering Rank: #90
Category / College Type: State Public University
Official Website: https://www.coeptech.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: MHT-CET, JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 50% aggregate (45% for reserved category)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: MHT-CET/JEE Main + CAP Counselling; Counseling Authority: State CAP Counselling
- Admission Process: MHT-CET/JEE Main -> CAP Registration -> Choice Filling -> Seat Allotment -> Reporting
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, MHT-CET/JEE Scorecard, Domicile Certificate, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹156,464
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹156,464
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹156,464
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 30,000-45,000 per year
- Room Types Available: Triple, Quadruple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: No
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 88%
- Highest Package: INR 52.57 LPA
- Average Package: INR 10 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Mercedes Benz, Tata
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: EBC/OBC/SC/ST Fee Waiver, Merit Scholarship, GoI Post-Matric Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: Maharashtra state government fee waiver schemes for EBC/OBC/SC/ST/VJNT candidates with family income below prescribed limits.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: Shivajinagar Campus - approx. 100 acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Pune Junction (approx. 4 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Shivajinagar Bus Stand (approx. 1 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: Mercedes Benz R&D, Tata Motors, Persistent Systems
