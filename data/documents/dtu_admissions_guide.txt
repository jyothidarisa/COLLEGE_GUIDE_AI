# Delhi Technological University (DTU)
Location: New Delhi, Delhi
NIRF Engineering Rank: #30
Category / College Type: State University (Public)
Official Website: https://dtu.ac.in/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 60
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with PCM and JEE Main qualification
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JAC Delhi; Counseling Authority: JAC Delhi
- Admission Process: Online application, entrance/merit selection, counselling, document verification, fee payment and admission confirmation
- Important Documents Required: Class 10 certificate, Class 12 marksheet, entrance-exam score/rank card, ID proof, photographs, category certificate if applicable, domicile certificate if applicable, medical certificate and allotment/admission letter

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹108,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹108,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹108,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: 75000
- Room Types Available: Single, double and shared rooms; Attached Bathrooms: Partial
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 93%
- Highest Package: 45
- Average Package: 1250000
- Top Recruiters: Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, TCS
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit, need-based, category/government, institute scholarships
- Scholarship Details: Tuition fee waivers and stipends based on merit, category and family income; MCM scholarship of Rs 1000-2000/month for eligible students

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 100 acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Delhi Sarai Rohilla Railway Station (8 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: DTU Bus Stop
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), 24×7 Campus Security, CCTV Surveillance, Women's Hostel Security, Anti-Sexual Harassment Cell, Emergency Helpline), Medical Support (24×7 Campus/Institute Hospital, Ambulance Service, Emergency Medical Care, Resident Doctors, Pharmacy, Counselling Services)
- Industry Collaborations: MoUs with leading companies, industry internships, joint projects, guest lectures, placement partnerships and industry-sponsored research
