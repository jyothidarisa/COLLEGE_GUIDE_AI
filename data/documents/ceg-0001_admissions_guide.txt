# Anna University (CEG-0001)
Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu
NIRF Engineering Rank: #20
Category / College Type: State University (Public)
Official Website: https://www.annauniv.edu/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: Class 12 Marks-based (No entrance exam)
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: Merit based on Class 12 marks
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: TNEA Counselling (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions); Counseling Authority: TNEA Counselling
- Admission Process: TNEA Application → Merit List → Counselling → Seat Allotment → Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, Community Certificate, Transfer Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Passport-size Photos
