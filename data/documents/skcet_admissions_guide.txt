# Sri Krishna College of Engineering and Technology (SKCET)
Location: Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu
NIRF Engineering Rank: #100
Category / College Type: Private Autonomous College
Official Website: https://www.skcet.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: TNEA (12th standard marks-based)
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: TNEA Counselling (Tamil Nadu); Counseling Authority: TNEA Counselling
- Admission Process: TNEA Registration -> Web Options -> Seat Allotment -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, TNEA Application Print, Transfer Certificate, Community Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹200,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹175,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹150,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 80,000-1,00,000 per year including mess
- Room Types Available: Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: No
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 97%
- Highest Package: INR 46 LPA
- Average Package: INR 6 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Infosys, Tech Mahindra, Capgemini, TCS, Cognizant
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: TNEA Merit Scholarship, First Graduate Scholarship, SC/ST Fee Reimbursement
- Scholarship Details: Tamil Nadu government scholarships and fee reimbursement schemes for SC/ST/first-generation graduate students.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 20 acres (Kuniamuthur, Coimbatore)
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Madukkarai Railway Station (approx. 6 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Ukkadam Bus Stand (approx. 8 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: Tech Mahindra, Capgemini, TCS iON
