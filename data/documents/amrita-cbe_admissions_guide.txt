# Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham (AMRITA-CBE)
Location: Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu
NIRF Engineering Rank: #23
Category / College Type: Deemed University (Private)
Official Website: https://www.amrita.edu/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: AEEE / JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 60% overall in Class 12
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: AEEE, JEE Main Score, Direct Admission; Counseling Authority: Amrita Admission Cell
- Admission Process: AEEE / JEE Main Score → Counselling → Seat Allotment → Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, AEEE/JEE Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Passport-size Photos, Medical Fitness Certificate
