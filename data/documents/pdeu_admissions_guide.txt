# Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU) (PDEU)
Location: Gandhinagar, Gujarat
NIRF Engineering Rank: #98
Category / College Type: Private University
Official Website: https://www.pdpu.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics, plus Chemistry/Biology/CS/Vocational
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Main Based (Merit Score); Counseling Authority: PDEU Counselling
- Admission Process: JEE Main Score -> Apply Online -> Merit-based Counselling -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JEE Main Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹300,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹250,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹200,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 85,000 per year + mess fee INR 44,000-52,000 per year
- Room Types Available: AC Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 85%
- Highest Package: INR 25 LPA
- Average Package: INR 6.09 LPA
- Top Recruiters: L&T, ONGC, Infosys, TCS, Reliance
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit Scholarship, JEE Rank Scholarship, SC/ST Fee Concession
- Scholarship Details: Tuition fee concessions for high JEE Main percentile scorers and category-based scholarships as per university policy.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 100 acres (Raisan, Gandhinagar)
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Gandhinagar Railway Station (approx. 8 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Gandhinagar Bus Stand (approx. 7 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: ONGC, Reliance Industries, L&T (Energy sector MoUs)
