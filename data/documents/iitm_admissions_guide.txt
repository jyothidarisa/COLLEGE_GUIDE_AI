# Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IITM)
Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu
NIRF Engineering Rank: #1
Category / College Type: Institute of National Importance
Official Website: https://www.iitm.ac.in/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Advanced
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 75% overall in Class 12
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics; Qualified in JEE Advanced
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Advanced, JoSAA Counselling; Counseling Authority: JoSAA
- Admission Process: JEE Advanced → JoSAA Counselling → Seat Allotment → Document Verification → Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th Mark Sheet, 12th Mark Sheet, JEE Scorecard, JoSAA Allotment Letter, Transfer Certificate (TC), Migration Certificate (if applicable), Character Certificate, Category Certificate (if applicable), PwD Certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar Card/Valid Photo ID, Passport-size Photographs, Medical Fitness Certificate
