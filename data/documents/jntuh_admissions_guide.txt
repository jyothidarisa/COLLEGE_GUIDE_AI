# JNTUH College of Engineering Hyderabad (JNTUH)
Location: Hyderabad, Telangana
NIRF Engineering Rank: #94
Category / College Type: State Public University
Official Website: https://www.jntuh.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: TS EAMCET
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: TS EAMCET + TS-EAPCET Counselling; Counseling Authority: TS-EAPCET Counselling
- Admission Process: TS EAMCET -> Web Options -> Seat Allotment -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, TS EAMCET Rank Card, Transfer Certificate, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹180,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹180,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹180,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 1,500-3,500 per month (with mess)
- Room Types Available: Sharing (up to 5 per room); Attached Bathrooms: No
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 72%
- Highest Package: INR 17 LPA
- Average Package: INR 5.5 LPA
- Top Recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Tech Mahindra, HCL
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Telangana ePASS Scholarship, SC/ST/OBC/BC/EBC Fee Reimbursement
- Scholarship Details: Telangana state ePASS scheme provides full tuition reimbursement and maintenance allowance for eligible category students.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: Approx. 100 acres (Kukatpally)
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Bharat Nagar Railway Station (approx. 7 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Kukatpally Bus Depot (approx. 6 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: TCS, Tech Mahindra, HCL
