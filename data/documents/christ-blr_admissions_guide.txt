# Christ University (Kengeri Campus, School of Engineering & Technology) (CHRIST-BLR)
Location: Bengaluru, Karnataka
NIRF Engineering Rank: #76
Category / College Type: Private Deemed University
Official Website: https://christuniversity.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: CUET (Christ), JEE Main (optional)
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 50% aggregate (45% for SC/ST)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics from a recognized board
- Admission Modes & Counseling: Entrance Test Based (Christ University Entrance Test) + JEE Main/CUET considered; Counseling Authority: Christ University Entrance Test Phase II Counselling
- Admission Process: Online application -> Entrance Test -> Merit List -> Counselling -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, Transfer Certificate, Migration Certificate, Character Certificate, Category Certificate (if applicable), Passport-size Photographs, Aadhaar Card/Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹300,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹225,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹212,500
- Hostel Accommodation Details: INR 60,000-1,20,000 per year depending on room type
- Room Types Available: Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 85%
