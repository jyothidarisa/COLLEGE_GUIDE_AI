# National Institute of Technology Meghalaya (NIT-MEGH)
Location: Shillong, Meghalaya
NIRF Engineering Rank: #83
Category / College Type: Institute of National Importance
Official Website: https://www.nitm.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 75% (65% SC/ST/PwD)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Main + JoSAA/CSAB Counselling; Counseling Authority: JoSAA/CSAB Special Round
- Admission Process: JEE Main -> JoSAA Registration -> Choice Filling -> Seat Allotment -> Reporting
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JEE Main Scorecard, JoSAA Allotment Letter, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹149,250
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹149,250
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹149,250
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 74,000-79,500 per year (hostel + mess + transport)
- Room Types Available: Single, Double, Triple, Quadruple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: No
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 78%
- Highest Package: INR 32 LPA
- Average Package: INR 10.4 LPA
- Top Recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Amazon, Bosch
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: SC/ST Fee Waiver, Merit-cum-Means Scholarship, North-East Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: 100% tuition waiver for SC/ST/PwD, income-based remission, and state-specific scholarships for North-East students.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: Temporary campus in Shillong (permanent campus under development at Sohra/Cherrapunji)
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Guwahati Railway Station (approx. 100 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Shillong Bus Stand (approx. 5 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: Bosch, TCS, regional IT/ITES companies
