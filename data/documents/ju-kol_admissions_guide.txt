# Jadavpur University (JU-KOL)
Location: Kolkata, West Bengal
NIRF Engineering Rank: #18
Category / College Type: State University (Public)
Official Website: https://jaduniv.edu.in/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: WBJEE
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 60% overall in Class 12
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: WBJEE, JEE Main Score, State Counselling; Counseling Authority: WBJEEB Counselling
- Admission Process: WBJEE → State Counselling → Seat Allotment → Document Verification → Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, WBJEE Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Domicile Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Passport-size Photos

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹15,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹15,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹15,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: 20000
- Room Types Available: Single, Double, Triple; Attached Bathrooms: no
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 90%
- Highest Package: ₹57 Lakhs domestic (international offers reported up to ₹85-140 Lakhs)
- Average Package: 11-12 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Google, Amazon, DE Shaw, Microsoft, TCS, Wipro, Schrodinger, Samsung, HSBC
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit Scholarship, State Government Scholarship, SC/ST/OBC Scholarship, EWS Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: Fee waivers and state-government-funded scholarships based on merit and family income for eligible students
