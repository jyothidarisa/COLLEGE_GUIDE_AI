# Indian Institute of Technology Bhubaneswar (IIT-BBS)
Location: Bhubaneswar, Odisha
NIRF Engineering Rank: #39
Category / College Type: Institute of National Importance
Official Website: https://www.iitbbs.ac.in/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Advanced
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 75
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with PCM; JEE Advanced qualification
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JoSAA; Counseling Authority: JoSAA
- Admission Process: Online application, entrance/merit selection, counselling, document verification, fee payment and admission confirmation
- Important Documents Required: Class 10 certificate, Class 12 marksheet, entrance-exam score/rank card, ID proof, photographs, category certificate if applicable, domicile certificate if applicable, medical certificate and allotment/admission letter

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹100,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹100,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹100,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: 50000
- Room Types Available: Single, double and shared rooms; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 85%
- Highest Package: 74
- Average Package: 1100000
- Top Recruiters: Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Deloitte, TCS
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit, need-based, category/government, institute scholarships
- Scholarship Details: Tuition fee waivers and stipends based on merit, category and family income; MCM scholarship of Rs 1000-2000/month for eligible students

- Campus Area: 100 acres
