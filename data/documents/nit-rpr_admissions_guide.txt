# National Institute of Technology Raipur (NIT-RPR)
Location: Raipur, Chhattisgarh
NIRF Engineering Rank: #86
Category / College Type: Institute of National Importance
Official Website: https://www.nitrr.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 75% (65% SC/ST/PwD)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Main + JoSAA/CSAB Counselling; Counseling Authority: JoSAA/CSAB Special Round
- Admission Process: JEE Main -> JoSAA Registration -> Choice Filling -> Seat Allotment -> Reporting
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JEE Main Scorecard, JoSAA Allotment Letter, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹125,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹125,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹125,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 43,000-45,500 per year (with mess)
- Room Types Available: Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 75%
- Highest Package: INR 60 LPA
- Average Package: INR 8.8 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Cold Forge, Jayaswal Neco Industries, TCS, Infosys
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: SC/ST Fee Waiver, Merit-cum-Means Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: 100% tuition waiver for SC/ST/PwD; income-based remission for economically weaker families as per MoE norms.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 100 acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Raipur Junction (approx. 15 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Raipur Bus Stand (approx. 15 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: Jayaswal Neco Industries, Cold Forge
