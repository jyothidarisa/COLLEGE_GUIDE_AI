# Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT-001)
Location: Vellore, Tamil Nadu
NIRF Engineering Rank: #16
Category / College Type: Deemed University (Private)
Official Website: https://vit.ac.in/

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: VITEEE / JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 60% overall in Class 12
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: VITEEE, JEE Main Score, Direct Admission; Counseling Authority: VIT Admission Cell
- Admission Process: VITEEE / JEE Main Score → Counselling → Seat Allotment → Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, Entrance Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Aadhaar Card, Passport-size Photos, Medical Fitness Certificate
