# Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad (IIT-DH)
Location: Dharwad, Karnataka
NIRF Engineering Rank: #77
Category / College Type: Institute of National Importance
Official Website: https://iitdh.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Main, JEE Advanced
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 75% (65% SC/ST/PwD)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics; qualified in JEE Advanced
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Advanced + JoSAA Counselling; Counseling Authority: JoSAA Counselling
- Admission Process: JEE Main -> JEE Advanced -> JoSAA Registration -> Choice Filling -> Seat Allotment -> Reporting
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JEE Advanced Scorecard, JoSAA Allotment Letter, Category Certificate (if applicable), Migration Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹200,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹200,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹200,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Hostel seat rent INR 1,000/sem, establishment INR 2,500/sem, mess advance approx. INR 20,000-24,500/sem
- Room Types Available: Double occupancy (1st year), Single (from 2nd year); Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 88%
- Highest Package: INR 52 LPA
- Average Package: INR 15 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Google, Oracle, Infosys, Microsoft, Samsung R&D, Bosch
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit-cum-Means Scholarship, SC/ST Fee Waiver, PM Scholarship Scheme
- Scholarship Details: 100% tuition waiver for SC/ST/PwD; 100% remission for family income below INR 1 lakh; 2/3rd remission for income INR 1-5 lakh.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 470 acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Dharwad Railway Station (approx. 12 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Dharwad Bus Stand (approx. 12 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: NRSC, Shilpa Biologicals, Aditya Birla Group
