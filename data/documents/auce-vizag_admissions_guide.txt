# Andhra University College of Engineering (A) (AUCE-VIZAG)
Location: Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh
NIRF Engineering Rank: #88
Category / College Type: State Public University (Autonomous College)
Official Website: https://www.andhrauniversity.edu.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: AP EAPCET, JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST)
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: AP EAPCET + State Counselling; JEE Main also considered for certain seats; Counseling Authority: AP State Engineering Counselling
- Admission Process: AP EAPCET -> Web Options -> Seat Allotment -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, EAPCET Rank Card, Transfer Certificate, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹200,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹200,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹178,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 31,450-33,850 per year
- Room Types Available: Double Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: No
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 75%
- Highest Package: INR 20 LPA
- Average Package: INR 6.04 LPA
- Top Recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture, DRDO
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Jagananna Vidya Deevena, SC/ST/OBC Fee Reimbursement, Merit Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: AP state government fee reimbursement schemes (Jagananna Vidya Deevena) for eligible category and income-group students.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: Andhra University North Campus - part of 422-acre main campus
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Visakhapatnam Railway Station (approx. 6 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: RTC Complex Visakhapatnam (approx. 6 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: DRDO, HPCL, Visakhapatnam Steel Plant
