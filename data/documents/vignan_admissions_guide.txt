# Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology and Research (VFSTR) (VIGNAN)
Location: Guntur, Andhra Pradesh
NIRF Engineering Rank: #80
Category / College Type: Private Deemed University
Official Website: https://vignan.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: V-SAT, JEE Main, AP EAPCET
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 50% aggregate
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: Entrance Test Based (V-SAT) + JEE Main/AP EAPCET considered; Counseling Authority: VFSTR Counselling / AP EAPCET Counselling
- Admission Process: Apply Online -> V-SAT/EAPCET Score -> Counselling -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, EAPCET/V-SAT Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹280,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹225,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹200,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 90,000-1,20,000 per year including mess
- Room Types Available: Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 90%
- Highest Package: INR 42 LPA
- Average Package: INR 4.5 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Infosys, Wipro, IBM, HCL, TCS, Amazon
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit Scholarship, EAPCET Rank Scholarship, SC/ST Fee Reimbursement
- Scholarship Details: Tuition fee concessions for high EAPCET/V-SAT rank holders and state government fee reimbursement for eligible categories.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 286 acres
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Chirala Railway Station (approx. 25 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Vadlamudi Bus Stand (approx. 1 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: Infosys Campus Connect, Wipro, IBM, TCS iON
