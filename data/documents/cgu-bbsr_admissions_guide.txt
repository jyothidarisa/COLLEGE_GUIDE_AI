# C.V. Raman Global University (CGU-BBSR)
Location: Bhubaneswar, Odisha
NIRF Engineering Rank: #95
Category / College Type: Private University
Official Website: https://cgu-odisha.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: CGU-CET, JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 45% aggregate
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: Entrance Test Based (CGU-CET) + JEE Main considered; Counseling Authority: CGU Counselling
- Admission Process: Apply Online -> CGU-CET/JEE Score -> Counselling -> Document Verification -> Fee Payment
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, CGU-CET/JEE Scorecard, Transfer Certificate, Category Certificate, Photo ID

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹225,000
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹175,000
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹140,000
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 35,000-1,60,000 per year (excl. mess)
- Room Types Available: AC/Non-AC Double, Triple Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: Yes
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 75%
- Highest Package: INR 45 LPA
- Average Package: INR 5.5 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Cognizant, IBM, Wipro, Accenture, TCS, Tech Mahindra
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Merit Scholarship, Single Girl Child Scholarship, SC/ST Fee Concession
- Scholarship Details: Merit-based scholarships for top entrance scorers, single girl child concession, and SC/ST fee support.

## 5. Campus Amenities and Connectivity
- Campus Area: 110 acres (Mahura, Bhubaneswar)
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
- Nearest Railway Station: Bhubaneswar Railway Station (approx. 14 km)
- Nearest Bus Stand: Baramunda Bus Stand (approx. 12 km)
- Women Safety & Emergency Support: Women Safety Cell (Yes), Medical Support (Yes)
- Industry Collaborations: Tata Steel, Bosch, TVS Motors
