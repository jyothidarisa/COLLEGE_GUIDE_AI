# University Institute of Engineering and Technology (UIET), Panjab University (UIET-PU)
Location: Chandigarh, Chandigarh
NIRF Engineering Rank: #93
Category / College Type: State Public University
Official Website: https://uiet.puchd.ac.in

## 1. Admissions and Eligibility
- Primary Entrance Exam: JEE Main
- Minimum PCM Eligibility: 60% aggregate
- Minimum Qualification: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
- Admission Modes & Counseling: JEE Main + JAC Chandigarh Counselling; Counseling Authority: JAC Chandigarh Counselling
- Admission Process: JEE Main -> JAC Chandigarh Registration -> Choice Filling -> Seat Allotment -> Reporting
- Important Documents Required: 10th & 12th Mark Sheets, JEE Main Scorecard, Category Certificate, Photo ID, Migration Certificate

## 2. Fee Structure and Living Costs
- CSE / AIML / IT Annual Tuition Fee: ₹336,800
- ECE Annual Tuition Fee: ₹336,800
- Mechanical / Civil Annual Tuition Fee: ₹336,800
- Hostel Accommodation Details: Approx. INR 12,000-14,000 per year
- Room Types Available: Single, Double Sharing; Attached Bathrooms: No
- Mess & Dining: Mess available (Yes)

## 3. Placements & Career Outcomes
- Placement Percentage: 78%
- Highest Package: INR 24.73 LPA
- Average Package: INR 8.5 LPA
- Top Recruiters: Philips India, Havells India, and 100+ recruiters
- Internship Assistance: Yes

## 4. Scholarships and Financial Aid
- Scholarships Available: Yes
- Scholarship Schemes: Panjab University Scholarship, State Scholarship, National Scholarship, SC/ST Scholarship
- Scholarship Details: University and state/national government scholarships for meritorious and economically weaker students, including SC/ST support.

- Campus Area: South Campus, Sector 25, Chandigarh
- Facilities: Library (Yes), Research Labs (Yes), Gym (Yes), Medical (Yes), Sports
